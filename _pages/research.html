Research

Learning and decision-making in wireless networks

I study how wireless devices can decide when to transmit and how to share limited resources. My work uses reinforcement learning and online learning to make these decisions under changing channel conditions, delay constraints, and limited energy.

Reinforcement learning for scheduling

I study how the structure of scheduling problems can guide reinforcement learning when transmission decisions must balance delay and resource use.

Distributed random access for the IoT

I investigate how devices can learn to share wireless channels using local feedback. My work includes evaluating learning-based access on real IoT hardware.

Bandits and online exploration

I explore how to balance trying unfamiliar actions with using what a system has already learned, especially when exploration consumes network resources.
